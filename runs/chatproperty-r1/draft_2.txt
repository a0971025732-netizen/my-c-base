The point that gets lost when people treat the token as its own asset is the one you lead with: tokenized real estate faces the same rate sensitivity as the physical property. Under rate pressure, what separates tokenized RE that holds from tokenized RE that cracks is the underlying market's real fundamentals — price trend, rental yield, how fast units actually clear. The wrapper doesn't change that; it just makes the exposure liquid. So the due-diligence question stays physical: what do actual transactions and rents say. ChatProperty on Finch does exactly that depth for one market — Singapore private residential, off 700k+ transactions and 1.1M+ rentals, with charts, 10 free runs.
