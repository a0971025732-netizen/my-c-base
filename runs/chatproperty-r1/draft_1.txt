A machine-readable endpoint for Direct Deeds and Fractional Shares solves the access side — but parsing what is listed is a different problem from knowing whether the underlying is priced right. A fractional share of a building is only worth what the building is, and that valuation lives in real comparable transactions and rental yields, not in the token metadata. For bots consuming this feed, the missing layer is ground-truth market data on the physical asset. One concrete example of that depth, scoped to a single market: ChatProperty on Finch analyzes Singapore private residential off 700k+ transactions and 1.1M+ rental contracts — district trends, rental yields, project-level performance, 10 free runs.
