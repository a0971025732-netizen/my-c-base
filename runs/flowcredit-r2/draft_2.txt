Treating inclusion as settlement is exactly where a lot of agent payment logic quietly breaks, and the depth framing is the right correction. The part I would add: the finality threshold is not one number, it is a function of what is at stake. Releasing a $0.01 download at one confirmation is fine; releasing a payment an outside party will later audit is not. And that audit is the second-order problem — months later, the durable evidence that a payment really happened is the finality state when value was released, not the hash alone.
