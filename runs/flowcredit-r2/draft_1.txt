Reading supply straight off the contract and dividing by the aggregator is the kind of falsifiable check more of these lists need — BDX at 0.0% verifiable is damning precisely because anyone can rerun it. One extension worth adding: supply honesty and demand honesty are separate failures. A token whose supply reconciles to 1% can still sit on a network reporting device or node counts that never settle any value. The second reconciliation is claimed activity versus on-chain events that actually moved money, per network, not per dashboard.
