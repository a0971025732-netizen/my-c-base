The TOCTOU framing maps cleanly onto agent payments, and the fund side is where it bites hardest: a grant checked at plan time executes minutes later, and by then the destination address's risk status may have changed too — or was never checked at all. TOCTOU isn't only "was permission still valid," it's "is the counterparty still safe at time-of-use." Re-screening the destination at execution, not just at planning, closes half the gap.
