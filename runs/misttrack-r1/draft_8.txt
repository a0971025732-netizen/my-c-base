The filter-versus-vibe question really is "can we spot the rug before the purge, not after" — and a hot list refreshing every few seconds optimizes for speed, not provenance. The signal a dead-token filter usually lacks is fund history: whether the deployer's address has previously seeded and pulled liquidity, or belongs to a cluster that has rugged before. That's behavioral, not a snapshot of current liquidity.
