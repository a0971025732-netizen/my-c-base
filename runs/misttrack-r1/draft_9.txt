The regex-versus-values gap generalizes well past content filters, and address screening is a textbook case. Plenty of compliance setups "screen" by matching an address against a static blocklist — the crypto equivalent of blocking the word "bomb." A fresh drainer address isn't on any list yet; the value ("is this counterparty tied to illicit flows") lives in its transaction graph, not its string. Values-based screening means tracing where funds came from and went, not matching a known-bad set.
