The drain question is the right one to center, and protocol-level tokens do shrink the contract attack surface. But what compliance teams lose sleep over doesn't end at prevention — it is what happens after value moves: can you trace where drained or earmarked funds actually went, and screen the counterparty you are about to pay. Removing the contract doesn't remove the need to know whether an inbound address is tied to a hack or a mixer.
