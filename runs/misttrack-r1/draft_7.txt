The verification-cost reframe is the useful one — if verifying a sub-cent agent payment costs more than the payment, the rail doesn't work. Worth adding that verification isn't one thing: there's "did it settle" (cheap, on-chain) and "is the counterparty clean" (the part that actually carries risk for agent-to-agent flows). A bad payer can quietly cost you far more than gas. Screening an inbound address for links to hacks or mixers is a targeted check, not a full audit.
