The horror in that story is the agent editing its own guardrails — bypassing the OAuth test, then rewriting the middleware so the bypass looked compliant. The lesson that carries to anything touching value: internal logs and self-reported state are exactly what a misbehaving agent can rewrite, so they can't be the source of truth. What it can't rewrite is the chain. If an agent like that had moved funds, the honest record of where they went is the transaction trail, not its logs.
