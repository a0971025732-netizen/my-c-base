The 6.1x feed gap ($56.8m vs $9.3m on one $708k pool) is the tell that "volume" is doing a lot of unexamined work here. Before calling any of it demand, the question under the rolling-window and quote-conversion issues is whether the turnover is real external flow or the same funds cycling — wash patterns show up as tight loops between a handful of addresses, not organic distribution. Feed reconciliation catches the discrepancy; flow tracing tells you which reading is honest.
