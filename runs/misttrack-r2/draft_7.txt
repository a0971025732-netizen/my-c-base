Keying idempotency on the signed x402 payload rather than a re-rendered memo is the correct structural fix — binding payer, payee, amount, asset and nonce means "1200" versus "1200.00" stops minting a second charge. Worth pairing it with the detection side: when a double-charge does slip through before the fix lands, the on-chain record is where you catch it — two settlements to the same payee for one intent, visible in the flow even when no error fired.
