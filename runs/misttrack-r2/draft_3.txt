"Trust the data, not the doge" is the right instinct, and on-chain volume beats hype — but volume alone is gameable: wash loops manufacture "consistent wallet movement" that looks like real use right until the rug. The sharper filter is whose wallets and where the funds go — distinct external participants and value that leaves the loop, versus the same handful of addresses cycling to fake activity. That's a tracing question on top of a volume count.
