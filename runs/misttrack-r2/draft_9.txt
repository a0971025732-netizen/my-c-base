The root issue you name — trust resting on user-defined metadata and self-authored descriptions — is a general pattern, and on-chain risk is the same mistake in another domain. An address, like a model card, presents whatever its owner wants; the trustworthy signal is not the self-description, it is the behavioral history you can independently verify. For a wallet that means its actual flows — what it received, from where, where it sent — not its label.
