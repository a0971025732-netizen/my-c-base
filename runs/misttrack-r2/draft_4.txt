Alerting on the earliest explainable signal is the right principle, and your three — transfer movement, contract-code context, nonce and queued-signer — are the obvious drift detectors. One worth adding to the priority list is destination risk: not just that value is moving or queued, but whether the address it is heading to is tied to a hack, a mixer, or a prior drain. A clean nonce to a dirty destination is still a bad outcome. That is a different lookup than movement or visibility — it is the counterparty's history.
