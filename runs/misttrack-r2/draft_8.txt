The ephemeral-agent case exposes a useful split: the identity that smooths an API's spend limits (who is this agent) is separate from the risk of the addresses that agent touches (who is it paying). Even a 60-second worker signing through an Identity Proxy can route value to a wallet tied to a hack — its own identity being clean says nothing about its counterparties. So "does identity matter" has two answers: the proxy solves friction; counterparty screening solves exposure.
