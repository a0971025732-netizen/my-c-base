"Every design flaw becomes a financial exposure" is the right reframe, and retry drains are only the visible half. The quieter exposure is who the agent pays: a loop with a bad exit condition that keeps paying is bad; a confident single payment to a counterparty tied to a drainer or mixer can be worse, and no amount of retry hygiene catches it. Architecture growing up includes screening destinations, not just bounding spend.
