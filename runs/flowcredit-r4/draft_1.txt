A dispute button is the right instinct, but it only bites if every line on the provenance card already carries its own evidence state — what independent record backs this transfer, who is allowed to contest it, and which facts are still only asserted. Your disclosure-date and evidence-scope fields are exactly that: they turn permanently-settled into settled as of this disclosure, against this evidence. The missing piece is a per-claim gap list — the card should say not just what is claimed but what a third party still cannot verify, so a dispute has something concrete to point at. That readiness pass — does each field trace to a record the counterparty cannot quietly edit, and what is missing before anyone relies on it — is assessable on its own.
