The unit-economics framing is right, and there is a second-order effect worth naming: the fixes for the gas floor — batching, off-chain netting, channel settlement — all move the real economic events off the chain. That solves the cost asymmetry but weakens auditability, because the on-chain trail no longer reconstructs actual per-call usage; you are back to trusting the operator's aggregation. So sub-cent economics and verifiable revenue pull in opposite directions unless you deliberately keep attributable settlement evidence at the netting boundary. The question is not only can we settle sub-cent profitably but can an outsider still reconstruct who paid for what after we batch it.
