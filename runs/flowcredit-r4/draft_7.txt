Pre-flight over post-mortem is the right axis — catching a stale nonce or slipped liquidity before the block beats observing the damage in high resolution. Worth separating two jobs that often get merged: the circuit breaker stops a bad action at execution time, but a third party auditing the operation months later needs the opposite artifact — durable evidence that the good actions actually settled, and against what state. Your gateway handles the first; the second is an after-the-fact evidence question, not a gate. They are complementary: block what should not run, and keep a reconstructable record of what did.
