The token-as-2017-ICO framing is fair, and native-currency settlement via x402 is the right correction to value-equals-mint-a-coin. The part worth adding: settling in USDC proves a payment moved, not that it came from a real external customer rather than a wallet you also control. Direct invoicing beats a token, but the credibility test is the same one that sinks most token projects — can an outsider reconstruct your revenue from settlement events and counterparties without trusting your word. Self-dealt USDC and real USDC look identical on-chain until someone traces the payers.
